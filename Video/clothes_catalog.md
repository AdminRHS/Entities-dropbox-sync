## Outfit Guidelines

### On-Camera Best Practices

**Colors:**
- ✅ Solid colors work best
- ✅ Blues, grays, earth tones
- ✅ Subtle patterns (small checks, fine stripes)
- ❌ Avoid pure white (overexposes)
- ❌ Avoid pure black (underexposes)
- ❌ Avoid busy patterns (moiré effect)
- ❌ Avoid bright neon (color bleed)

**Fit:**
- Comfortable (you'll be wearing it for hours)
- Not too tight (wrinkles, movement restriction)
- Not too loose (sloppy appearance)
- Flattering for camera angle

**Style Consistency:**
- Match video tone (casual vs professional)
- Maintain brand identity
- Consider audience expectations
- Stay authentic to personal style

## Preparation Checklist

**Day Before Shoot:**
- [ ] Select outfit based on video type
- [ ] Check for stains/damage
- [ ] Check for wrinkles
- [ ] Iron/steam if needed
- [ ] Prepare backup outfit (different style)
- [ ] Try on to verify fit/comfort
- [ ] Check accessories (belt, watch, etc.)
- [ ] Set aside, ready to grab

**Shoot Day:**
- [ ] Put on outfit 30 min before leaving
- [ ] Final mirror check
- [ ] Bring backup in bag
- [ ] Lint roller check
- [ ] Comfortable for entire shoot duration
