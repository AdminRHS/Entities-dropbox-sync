# Videographer

**Department:** Video Production
**Level:** Mid
**Entity:** VID
**Code:** VID.VIDEOGRAPHER

---

## Overview

Videographers are responsible for camera operation, shooting execution, and capturing high-quality footage according to the production plan.

---

## Responsibilities

### Primary
- Execute video shoots according to shot list
- Operate camera equipment professionally
- Set up and adjust lighting
- Monitor audio levels during recording
- Ensure footage quality and consistency
- Manage memory cards and footage organization

### Secondary
- Scout locations for shoots
- Recommend equipment for specific projects
- Basic on-site editing/review
- Equipment maintenance and care
- Train junior team members

---

## Skills Required

### Technical Skills
- Camera operation (DSLR, mirrorless, cinema cameras)
- Lighting setup and adjustment
- Audio recording basics
- Exposure control (aperture, shutter, ISO)
- Focus techniques (manual and auto)
- Stabilization (tripod, gimbal, handheld)
- Shot composition and framing
- Color temperature/white balance

### Soft Skills
- Attention to detail
- Time management
- Problem-solving under pressure
- Communication with directors
- Physical stamina (long shoots)
- Adaptability to conditions

---

## Tools Used

### Software

---

## Daily Workflow

### Pre-Shoot
1. Review shot list and requirements
2. Check and pack equipment
3. Charge all batteries
4. Format memory cards
5. Travel to location early

### During Shoot
1. Set up equipment systematically
2. Test all settings before recording
3. Execute shot list methodically
4. Monitor footage quality continuously
5. Communicate with director/team
6. Backup footage if possible

### Post-Shoot
1. Review all footage captured
2. Upload to Google Drive (see upload_workflow.md)
3. Create local backup
4. Clean and store equipment
5. Log shoot details
6. Note any issues for future

---

## Performance Metrics

### Quality Metrics
- Footage usability rate (target: >95%)
- Technical quality score (exposure, focus, audio)
- Shot list completion rate (target: 100%)

### Efficiency Metrics
- Setup time (target: <30 min)
- Shots per hour captured
- On-time shoot completion
- Equipment readiness

## Common Challenges

### Challenge: Low Light Conditions
**Solution:**
- Increase ISO (watch for noise)
- Open aperture wider
- Add artificial lighting
- Use faster lenses

### Challenge: Unstable Footage
**Solution:**
- Use tripod/gimbal
- Proper handheld technique
- Higher shutter speed
- Post-stabilization

### Challenge: Time Pressure
**Solution:**
- Prioritize critical shots
- Efficient setup procedures
- Pre-planned contingencies
- Clear communication
