# HR Recruiter

**Department:** Human Resources
**Level:** Mid
**Entity:** HR
**Code:** HR.RECRUITER

---

## Overview

HR Recruiters source, screen, and evaluate candidates for open positions, managing the hiring pipeline from job posting to offer acceptance.

---

## Responsibilities

### Primary
- Source candidates through multiple channels
- Screen applications and resumes
- Conduct initial phone/video interviews
- Coordinate AI-assisted video interviews
- Evaluate candidates against job requirements
- Schedule interviews with hiring managers
- Manage candidate communication
- Maintain applicant tracking system
- Conduct reference checks
- Prepare offer letters

### Secondary
- Build talent pipeline for future needs
- Attend job fairs and networking events
- Maintain relationships with candidates
- Improve recruitment processes
- Analyze hiring metrics
- Employer branding initiatives

## Daily Workflow

### Morning (9:00-12:00)
1. Check Monday.com for urgent tasks
2. Review new applications from overnight
3. Screen resumes (initial filter)
4. Send rejection/next step emails
5. Schedule phone screens for promising candidates
6. Conduct 2-3 phone screens (15-20 min each)

### Afternoon (13:00-17:00)
1. Source new candidates (LinkedIn, job boards)
2. Review AI video interview analyses
3. Coordinate with hiring managers
4. Conduct detailed candidate interviews (30-45 min)
5. Update ATS with all activities
6. Prepare candidate packages for managers
7. Follow up with pending candidates

### Weekly Tasks
- Review hiring pipeline health
- Report metrics to HR Manager
- Attend team meetings
- Update job postings
- Nurture passive candidates

## AI-Assisted Video Interviews

### Workflow Integration

**Setup Phase:**
1. Candidate applies
2. Recruiter reviews resume
3. If qualified, send video interview link
4. AI generates custom questions based on:
   - Job requirements
   - Resume content
   - Company culture

**Interview Phase:**
1. Candidate records responses (async)
2. AI analyzes:
   - Communication skills
   - Technical knowledge depth
   - Cultural fit indicators
   - Response completeness
   - Red flags or inconsistencies
3. AI generates summary report

**Review Phase:**
1. Recruiter reviews AI analysis
2. Watches flagged sections of video
3. Makes human judgment call
4. Decides: Reject / Phone screen / Advance

**Benefits:**
- 24/7 candidate availability
- Standardized evaluation
- Bias reduction
- Time efficiency (pre-screening)
- Detailed analysis documentation

## Candidate Pipeline Stages

### 1. Sourcing
- Post job openings
- Search LinkedIn/job boards
- Employee referrals
- Passive candidate outreach

### 2. Initial Screening
- Resume review
- Application questions
- ATS scoring
- Quick qualification check

### 3. Phone Screen (15-20 min)
- Verify basic qualifications
- Assess communication
- Discuss salary expectations
- Gauge interest level
- Next steps explanation

### 4. AI Video Interview
- Automated question delivery
- AI analysis
- Recruiter review of results
- Decision: proceed or pass

### 5. Hiring Manager Interview
- In-depth technical/role assessment
- Team fit evaluation
- Detailed experience review

### 6. Final Interviews
- Team interviews
- Executive approval (if required)
- Culture fit assessment

### 7. Offer Stage
- Reference checks
- Background check
- Offer preparation
- Negotiation
- Acceptance

### 8. Pre-Onboarding
- Paperwork collection
- Equipment ordering
- Welcome communication
- Handoff to onboarding specialist
